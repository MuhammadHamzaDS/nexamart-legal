Conditions for using NexaMart WhatsApp shopping and support · Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. About the service

NexaMart AI Support is a conversational shopping and support interface connected to the NexaMart ecommerce backend. Automated responses assist with product information, carts, checkout, orders and customer support. The underlying store records are authoritative.

2. Product information and availability

Prices, product specifications, availability and estimated delivery fees are subject to confirmation by NexaMart at the time of checkout. Product or AI descriptions may contain errors; the confirmed order summary is the reference for your purchase.

3. Orders and confirmation

Adding products to a cart or requesting checkout does not by itself create an order. You must review and expressly confirm the final order summary. An order is placed only if the NexaMart backend accepts it and provides a genuine order record/identifier. A confirmation message is not a substitute for backend acceptance.

4. Shipping and payment

Available shipping and payment methods depend on the store’s current options and the customer’s location. Cash on delivery and bank-transfer instructions, where offered, follow the displayed store terms. Do not send payment credentials or sensitive identity documents to the chat assistant.

5. Cancellations, returns and support

Order cancellation, returns and refunds depend on the applicable NexaMart store policies and the current order status. A customer-support request is not an automatic approval of a refund or cancellation. Contact customer support for clarification.

6. Responsible use

Do not attempt unauthorized access to other accounts, provide false delivery details, misuse the assistant, or send illegal or harmful content. NexaMart may limit access to protect users and the service.

7. Automated assistance

The assistant can misunderstand requests. Ask for a human support representative if an automated reply is unclear or you need additional help. Actions affecting an order require valid store checks and any applicable confirmation.
