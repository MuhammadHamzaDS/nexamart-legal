NexaMart Customer Support

Privacy Policy

WhatsApp, shopping, orders, and customer-support data · Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how NexaMart AI Support handles information in connection with its shopping and customer-service workflows, including its WhatsApp assistant and connected NexaMart store. It should be read alongside WhatsApp/Meta’s own privacy information.

2. Information we may process

  • Contact details such as the WhatsApp phone number and profile/display name provided through the service.
  • Messages you send and replies from the assistant, along with technical delivery and error information.
  • Product searches, selected items, cart information, account-linking status and customer-support requests.
  • When you choose checkout: customer name, delivery address, phone number, shipping choice, payment-method selection and order information.
  • Information needed to verify account ownership before showing private account, cart or order information.

3. Why information is used

To respond to enquiries, search products, manage carts, prepare and place orders after confirmation, provide order updates where enabled, handle support cases, prevent duplicate or unauthorized requests, maintain service reliability, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. AI processing and service providers

Some requests may be processed using third-party language-model services (such as Groq) and infrastructure or messaging services, including the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Information necessary for a requested operation may also be passed to the NexaMart ecommerce backend and relevant delivery or payment providers. We aim to limit information shared to what is needed for these purposes.

5. Orders and payments

Order and shipping data may need to be retained to process purchases, handle returns and disputes, and satisfy applicable record-keeping obligations. The assistant should not request full card credentials through chat; if online payment is available, follow the approved payment flow presented by NexaMart.

6. Access and security

Private cart, account, and order operations require appropriate identity verification. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share account passwords, one-time codes or card details in WhatsApp chat unless specifically required by a trusted official verification flow.

7. Retention and your choices

Records may be kept for the time reasonably needed for the stated purposes and applicable legal requirements. You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible data, or ask about WhatsApp communication preferences. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features or processing arrangements change. Contact: EMAIL.
