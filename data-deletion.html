Data Deletion Instructions

Instructions for requesting access, correction, or deletion · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Request deletion or correction

If you have used NexaMart AI Support through WhatsApp or the NexaMart store, you can request deletion or correction of personal information associated with your account or phone number.

  1. Send an email to REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with subject NexaMart Data Deletion Request.
  2. Explain whether you want your WhatsApp assistant chat data, a linked-account connection, or other eligible personal information reviewed for deletion.
  3. Provide enough information to identify the relevant account, such as the WhatsApp phone number used or an order reference. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or payment-card numbers.
  4. The support team will verify that the request comes from an authorized account holder and tell you which information can be removed, corrected or retained for legal and transaction-record purposes.

What may be retained

Some order, payment, fraud-prevention, audit or support records may need to remain for applicable legal obligations or legitimate unresolved transactions. Where deletion is not possible, we will explain the reason where appropriate.

Unlinking WhatsApp

If an account-linking/unlinking option is available in the NexaMart account area, you can also use it to remove access for future private chat operations. Unlinking does not automatically delete prior completed orders.

Contact and follow-up

For privacy questions or to follow up on a deletion request, contact EMAIL.
